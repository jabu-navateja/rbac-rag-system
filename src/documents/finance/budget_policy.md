# Finance Budget Policy

Department: Finance
Document Type: Policy
Access Level: Full

The annual operating budget is approved by the Finance Director and executive leadership.

Department managers may submit budget requests during the annual planning cycle.

Budget requests above $50,000 require Finance Director approval.

Budget requests above $250,000 require executive committee approval.

Quarterly budget reviews are conducted by the Finance department.

Budget variance above 10% must be documented and reviewed by the Finance Director.

Employees may view general budget procedures but cannot approve or modify organizational budgets.
