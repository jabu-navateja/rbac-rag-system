# Finance Expense Policy

Department: Finance
Document Type: Policy
Access Level: Full

Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

Receipts are required for individual expenses above $25.

Managers can approve employee expense claims up to $5,000.

Finance reviews expense claims for policy compliance.

Expenses above $5,000 require Finance department approval.

Fraudulent or intentionally incorrect expense claims may result in disciplinary action.
