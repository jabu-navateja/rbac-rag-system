# Legal Contract Policy

Department: Legal
Document Type: Policy
Access Level: Full

All company contracts must be reviewed by the Legal department before execution.

Contracts must identify the parties, obligations, duration, termination conditions, and applicable law.

Business teams may request contract reviews through the Legal department.

Contracts involving financial commitments above $100,000 require additional Legal and executive review.

Employees must not modify or sign company contracts unless they have been specifically authorized to do so.

Executed contracts must be stored in the approved company repository.
