# Legal Compliance Policy

Department: Legal
Document Type: Policy
Access Level: Full

The company maintains compliance procedures covering data protection, contractual obligations, and regulatory requirements.

Employees must follow all applicable company compliance policies.

Potential compliance violations must be reported through the approved internal reporting process.

The Legal department reviews significant compliance incidents.

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams complete required compliance training.

Compliance records must be maintained according to the company's document retention requirements.
