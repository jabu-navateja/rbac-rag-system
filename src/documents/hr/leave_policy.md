# HR Leave Policy

Department: HR
Document Type: Policy
Access Level: Full

Employees are eligible for annual leave, sick leave, and other approved types of leave according to company policy.

Annual leave requests should normally be submitted through the employee leave management system.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests for their team members.

Employees should provide reasonable notice for planned leave whenever possible.

Sick leave may require supporting documentation according to company policy.

The HR department maintains the official leave policy and handles policy-related questions.
