# HR Employee Records

Department: HR
Document Type: Employee Records
Access Level: Own Records Only

Employee records contain information related to an individual's employment with the company.

Records may include employee identification information, job title, department, joining date, leave information, and performance-related information.

Employees may access information belonging to their own employee record through approved HR systems.

Employees must not access or request private records belonging to other employees.

Managers may access employee information required for legitimate management responsibilities.

HR administrators may access employee records according to their authorized responsibilities.

Confidential employee information must not be disclosed to unauthorized users.
